**DP158 boundary check:** A theorem-backed conservative fixed-order RDP *reference* filter is implemented and unit-tested, but is not a certified production accountant. A separate million-path-per-arm sweep found max Gaussian-CDF discrepancy **0.01463 → 0.02815 → 0.04813 → 0.09218** at `q=0.1,0.19,0.3,0.5`, while halt rates remained near 99%. The three increases met the predeclared materiality rule after simultaneous Monte Carlo uncertainty; this confirms a finite-`q` limitation already anticipated by the paper, not a novel privacy failure. [Boundary analysis](results/dp158_regime_boundary/summary.json).
