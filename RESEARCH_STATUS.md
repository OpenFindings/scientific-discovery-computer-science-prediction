**EDCS mathematical checkpoint (2026-10-15):** The four-edge witness was independently replayed without importing the runner. Its queue and degree arithmetic show that update 4 must add the P2-deficient edge (1,0), after which the old edge (0,0) has degree sum 5>4. For every integer beta=b>=3 with beta_minus=b-1, the same star-plus-cross-edge construction proves that this one-toggle model needs at least two single-edge H toggles, including the beta=3 boundary and every possible one-toggle outcome. An adversarial review corrected an overbroad generic lemma: with beta_minus=2, beta=4, an alternate unused edge can repair a deficient edge without inserting that edge. The surviving theorem is restricted to the explicit one-toggle/private-endpoint assumptions and is not a general dynamic-EDCS complexity lower bound. Bernstein–Stein's alternating full/deficient paths and bounded O(beta) repair work are known prior art, as are later HEDCS and EDCS verification/repair uses. EDCS is therefore closed as an independently verified educational/engineering result, not a publishable breakthrough.
