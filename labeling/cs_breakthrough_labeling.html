CS breakthrough labeling

Judge each paper's contribution, as seen about 3 years after its publication year. You may read the paper or search the web, but please do not look up citation counts: they are what this labeling is checking. Labels save in this browser automatically. When you're done, click Export labels and send the downloaded JSON file back.
Rubric
  • 3 – Breakthrough: opened a new direction, or became a widely adopted method, benchmark, system or result within ~3 years.
  • 2 – Significant: a solid contribution that others clearly built on, but not field-changing.
  • 1 – Incremental: a modest or narrow contribution with little visible uptake.
  • 0 – Can't judge: outside your expertise, or not enough information.
