# CS breakthrough radar

This is a ranked research-work queue, not a list of proven breakthroughs. The order prioritizes testability and information gained per unit effort. Paper 3 scores are unavailable until leakage-free historical CS outcomes are built; Paper 4 scores remain explicit planning estimates, not validated success probabilities.

The separate frozen 20-paper historical cohort has 13 source-grounded questions after exact-version section screening, but none yet has a verified direct follow-up label. Its formal-algorithm, security, database and software-testing questions are candidates for the next evidence search, not a re-ranking of the 155–158 opportunity list or a validated breakthrough.

The [bottleneck audit](historical_label_bottleneck_audit.json) changes the work queue: only 4/13 old questions had checked future primary full-text sections; the other nine are primarily retrieval-uncertain. The four checked near-misses fail exact comparability, so repeating the same broad keywords has low immediate value. A separately frozen 16-title cohort was screened under a multi-dimensional trajectory rubric. **16/16 source sections are now checked**: 12 questions are eligible, four papers are ineligible, all 12 eligible questions have bounded future-evidence audits, and **zero direct outcomes** were authenticated. A targeted audit of the 2004.11573 weak retrieval upgraded it to a dated full-text near-match, but the follow-up used a different intervention, comparator and budget; it remains unadjudicated. The trajectory dataset contains five partial-progress rows, one independent broad-premise strengthening row and three scope-narrowing rows; these are not direct labels. The observed yield is far too small to estimate Paper 3 performance.

Initial post-lock triage of 13 questions found a useful boundary: a 2024 polynomial algorithm for *unmarked* fixed-k minimum-sum cycle packing does not settle the 2021 *marked-vertex* three-cycle problem or its RNC/randomized-time target. This is a novelty-bounded formal-algorithm lead only. The 13 unadjudicated questions require deeper direct-source verification; zero direct labels exist.

### Latest feasibility update

The [CAM artifact-ablation audit](cam_artifact_ablation_feasibility.json) is a conditional **no-go** with currently verified public data: the original industrial CAM logs/labels are not jointly released with the paired software artifacts needed for the added-artifact arm, and the independent Ericsson result is binary product-fault recall rather than the source's multiclass cause endpoint. This is a completed feasibility decision, not a performance result. The next affordable candidate is the Badger dependency/metric gate, still before any matched-CPU claim.

## Paper 3 and Paper 4 status

No grouped chronological holdout, AUC, calibration or breakthrough probability is valid. Do not reuse astronomy effect sizes.
- Paper 4: the documented evidence-graph/action-ranking method can structure tests, but no historical action-level ground truth is available here. Do not call expected utility observed scientific value.
- New low-cost candidates: Avocado constrained-CIT follow-up is partial progress on a planned solver capability but still lacks a matched unconstrained baseline; SERENIoT follow-up narrows the decisive gap to cross-device whitelist generalization and adversarial false-allow/false-block rates. Both need endpoint-matched experiments before ranking above Badger/CAM.
- Repair-explanation transfer: the [artifact-only feasibility pilot](patch_explanation_feasibility.json) passes synthetic blinding/material checks, but the local environment has no Defects4J CLI or usable Java runtime. No real benchmark or human-review effect was measured.
- Historical outcome audit: one retrieval gap was upgraded to a dated full-text near-match, but exact intervention/comparator/budget mismatch preserved `unadjudicated` for all 13 questions; no Paper 3 score is allowed.
- Uncertainty-robustness feasibility: the source generator is algorithmically described, but no version-pinned implementation, data/model bundle, matched comparator or equal-compute protocol was authenticated; current direct-test decision is a bounded no-go, not an efficacy result.
- Candidate selection changed after the matched-CPU negative bug-yield checkpoint: DBMS is now below finding 158. Do not extend the DBMS run until it turns positive; any new campaign needs a fresh protocol and a distinct-bug outcome.
- The new [Badger action graph](paper4_badger_action_plan.json) is an offline Paper 4-style plan with declared illustrative likelihoods; neither its expected information gain nor its ranking has been validated against an executed action. The immediate priority is feasibility, not an asserted breakthrough.
