# Preregistration: SQLancer++ feedback operational replication

- **Study ID:** `EXP-DBMS-FEEDBACK-REP-4111-4115`
- **Registered:** 2026-10-13 UTC, before inspecting these seeds' outcomes.
- **Evidence tier target:** Level 4 for a reproducible operational measurement only; not a new-bug or scientific-agent result.
- **Prior evidence:** Existing `sqlancerpp_feedback_long_campaign` used seeds 4101–4105 and found five positive feedback-on minus feedback-off differences (215, 221, 373, 277, 244) in estimated successful query pairs under a 20-database cap. It was selected after a shorter null, and therefore is exploratory and vulnerable to seed-selection/measurement artifacts.
- **Question:** Does the same feedback toggle produce a positive successful-query-pair difference on five previously unused, paired seeds under the same pinned SQLancer++ build, SQLite 3.40.0, NoREC oracle, Docker image, per-condition 20-database cap, and 200-query-pair cap per database?
- **H0:** Across these five seeds, feedback-on is not consistently higher than feedback-off on the operational proxy.
- **H1:** Feedback-on is higher on all five paired seeds. This strict sign criterion is predeclared to make the small confirmation set difficult to over-interpret.
- **Seeds:** 4111–4115, paired within seed; selected as the next unused contiguous block, not by observed outcome.
- **Arms:** SQLancer++ `feedback_off` and `feedback_on`. No cross-tool comparison, no combined QPG × validity-feedback factorial, no claim about defect yield.
- **Primary outcome:** Difference in number of successful query pairs between arms, derived from retained per-database success-rate outputs multiplied by the fixed 200 attempts/database. Store every raw rate and derived numerator. Source inspection after the run confirmed the pinned logger prints the direct `(double) qsuccess / queryNum` ratio (`src/sqlancer/general/GeneralErrorHandler.java:183`), so the numerator is reconstructed exactly for this build; it remains an operational proxy, not a bug outcome.
- **Secondary outcomes:** completed databases, process exits, elapsed wall time, and exact per-database trace/measurement availability. Do not use runtime as matched compute; this is matched caps, not matched CPU time.
- **Analysis:** Report all five paired differences, sign count, mean/median/range, and exact two-sided sign-test p-value as descriptive (all five signs in one direction gives 0.0625 two-sided; one-sided directional p=0.03125). No exclusions except a predeclared incomplete pair; preserve all failed runs and report them. No optional stopping or additional seed selection based on intermediate results.
- **Falsification/decision gate:** The specific “consistent operational lift transfers to fresh seeds” lead is weakened if any valid seed is zero/negative, or if any direction depends on rounded/incomplete rates. Regardless of direction, do not infer more bugs, better research planning, or AI-agent effectiveness. A next study must use exact numerator logs and matched CPU-hours, then deduplicate and independently reproduce NoREC discrepancies.
- **Reproducibility:** `scripts/run_sqlancerpp_long_campaign.py --source /private/tmp/sqlancerpp-source-1405d4e --output results/sqlancerpp_feedback_replication_4111_4115 --first-seed 4111 --seeds 5 --database-cap 20`; source commit and container digest must match prior `metadata.json` records. Docker containers run with `--network none` and one CPU.
- **Compute ceiling:** 10 bounded container runs; one CPU/container; 20 databases × 200 query-pair attempts per condition; stop immediately on source/image/version mismatch or a measurement/schema failure. No paid model calls.
